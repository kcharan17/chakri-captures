Behind the lens

Chakri is a photographer and videographer from Vignan, Visakhapatnam. He works with a simple idea: every frame should carry a feeling. From festivals and go-kart races to quiet moments in nature, he tells each story with clean composition, rich colour and a cinematic finish.
